Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

LumaScore reads and plays sheet music on your iPhone or iPad. Your scores, photos, notes, and audio stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them. No account is required. LumaScore has no ads, tracking, or remote analytics. Settings and performance data stay on your device.

Files and camera

LumaScore opens only files you choose and leaves the originals unchanged. If you import from cloud storage, your provider may download the file under its privacy policy.

Scanning uses the camera and omits location, capture date, and device details from photos. LumaScore does not access your microphone or photo library. You can turn off camera access in Settings and still import files.

Saving and sharing

Choose Close score or open another score to discard the temporary copy. LumaScore clears interrupted imports the next time it opens. Save score keeps the sheet, notes, and edits in your Library until you delete them. Temporary imports and saved scores are excluded from device backups.

Exported copies stay wherever you save or share them, even if you delete LumaScore. Delete those copies in Files or the receiving service. That service’s privacy policy applies.

Trial and subscriptions

Your device’s Keychain stores your trial start date and daily page count. These records may survive reinstallation. Apple handles payments through your Apple Account. LumaScore uses Apple’s StoreKit to verify access.

Contact and privacy requests

Websites you open from LumaScore receive ordinary connection details. The app never attaches scores to links or support emails automatically.

Email lumascore@gmail.com with privacy questions or to request deletion of support messages. We receive your email address and whatever you send. Gmail handles those messages under Google’s privacy policy.

We update this notice and our App Store disclosures when our privacy practices change.
