Scanning and reading

How do I read my first score?

Tap Scan score to photograph a page or Import PDF to choose a PDF, JPEG, or PNG from Files. Tap Read score. Keep LumaScore open while it reads. You can minimize the reading panel to see the sheet.

How can I get a clearer reading from a photo?

Keep the page flat and the camera parallel to it. Include both ends of every staff, use even light, and check that thin lines look sharp. If the sheet is on a computer screen, importing the original PDF avoids screen reflections and stripe patterns.

What if reading pauses or a page fails?

Check the message at the bottom of the sheet. If the device is hot, let it cool, then tap Resume when available. For a failed page, tap Retry. Pages already read remain available.

Playback and editing

How do I compare a wrong note with the original?

Choose Split to see the printed page beside the recognized score. Both cursors follow the same musical position. After reading finishes, tap the cursor to inspect or change the notes and their durations.

How do I practice just my part?

Switch from All to Play chosen notes. In Score view, tap individual notes, or tap Select range and choose the first and last notes on the same staff. You can scroll between taps. Press Play to hear your selection.

Can I slow down or repeat a passage?

Open playback options to change the tempo in beats per minute. Turn on the loop button in the playback bar to repeat playback.

Library and exports

How do I keep a score and my edits?

Open the three-dot menu and choose Save score. Give it a name and choose a folder. To find it again, open Library on the home screen.

Which files can I export?

With LumaScore Plus, open Export and choose WAV or MP3 for audio, or MusicXML for editable notation. Select all notes or your chosen notes, then share the file or save it to Files. Export only music you have the right to share.

Trial, subscriptions, and privacy

What does the free trial include?

The trial lasts seven days and lets you read up to 15 pages per day. Only successfully read pages count. Saved scores remain available after the trial. Plus unlocks unlimited reading and export.

How do I restore or cancel a subscription?

Use Restore purchases in LumaScore with the Apple Account you used to subscribe. To manage renewal, open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple’s subscription guide.

Where does my music go?

Recognition and playback run on your device and require no LumaScore account. You choose whether to export a file or share it with someone. Read the privacy policy for details about storage, subscriptions, and support email.
